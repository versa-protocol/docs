# No Active Billing Contract

Versa returns this error with status `402 Payment Required` and code `no_active_contract`.

This error means there is no active billing agreement for your organization. It usually means a contract period has expired without renewal, and can happen automatically at the end of a pilot or trial period.

## Example Response

```json
{
  "type": "https://docs.versa.org/errors/no-active-contract",
  "title": "No active billing contract",
  "status": 402,
  "code": "no_active_contract",
  "detail": "This account does not have an active billing contract. Update your billing details or renew your contract to restore access."
}
```

## How to Resolve

Update your billing details or renew your contract, or contact us at support@versa.org. Rejected requests are not recorded, so resend them once your contract is active.
